## Key Principles (Reference Often)

### Mission
"You don't have to know everything, and you don't have to do this alone."

### Brand Voice
Simple. Preparedness. Planning.
- Approachable, not distant
- Psychologically attuned
- Strategically light (serious without alarmism)
- Author invisible (sourced, not personality-driven)

### Moderation Philosophy
Assume good intent. Extend grace. Correct rarely. Remove only when necessary.

### Culture Builder
Your role is to facilitate, celebrate, and welcome—not to control or police.

## Quick Troubleshooting

**Not many people introducing themselves?**
- Post more in #first-steps yourself
- Model what you want to see

**Questions not getting answered?**
- Answer yourself first (show that questions matter)
- Invite others to help
- This builds a learning culture

**Someone posts alarmist content?**
- Private message first
- Assume good intent
- Ask about their source

**Feeling overwhelmed?**
- You have moderators—use them
- First month is intensive, then settles
- Focus on welcome, not perfection
