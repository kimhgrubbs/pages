# SAL Discord - 30-Day Community Building Strategy

This is your playbook for the first month. The goal is to build psychological safety, establish community norms, and create self-sustaining conversation momentum.

**Timeline:** Sept 15 (Launch) → Oct 15 (30-day mark)

---

## Week 1: Welcome & Trust Building (Sept 15-21)

### Goal
Make people feel welcome and understood. Establish psychological safety. Prove that this is a real community, not a sales funnel or content silo.

### Moderator Actions

**Daily (Morning Check-In, ~30 min)**
- Welcome every new member who introduced themselves in #introductions
- Thank them specifically for what they shared (show you read it)
- If they mentioned something concrete, acknowledge it: *"Love that you're checking baseline prices. That clarity is exactly what we're building."*
- Answer any questions posted in #ask-anything (or invite others to help)

**Daily (Afternoon Engagement, ~30 min)**
- Respond to posts in #market-discussion, #first-steps, #planning-together
- Acknowledge observations: *"That's exactly the kind of local insight we need."*
- Don't agree/disagree—validate that they're noticing something real
- Ask one follow-up question to deepen the thread

**3x Per Week (Deep Engagement)**
- Post a moderator story in #introductions (Tuesday): "Hi, I'm [name]. What brought me to SAL was [personal story]. One thing I'm doing this week..."
- This models vulnerability and makes you real, not authoritarian
- Comment on interesting discussions with genuine curiosity
- Repost best #ask-anything questions into relevant channels (with person's permission)

**Weekly (Friday Synthesis)**
- Post a comment in #market-discussion: "This week I noticed... What did you all see?"
- Pulls together the week's observations
- Creates a rhythm

### Content Drops

**Monday (Post in #resources)**
- One USDA report or commodity data link
- Brief SAL perspective: *"Why this matters to supply resilience"*

**Wednesday (Post in #decision-making)**
- Short framework post: "Making decisions without perfect information"
- Reference: System 1/System 2 thinking, or "how anxiety affects decisions"
- Keep it 2-3 paragraphs. Actionable.

**Friday (Post in #supply-chain-alerts)**
- Mid-week update if anything major happened
- Otherwise, end-of-week summary

**As Needed**
- If someone shares a concrete win in #first-steps, celebrate it in #wins-and-progress with their name
- If someone asks a really good question, highlight it: *"[Name] asked this in #ask-anything and it's worth everyone thinking about..."*

### Metrics to Watch
- **Introduction rate:** You want 5-10 new members introducing themselves by Friday
- **Ask-anything questions:** 3-5 questions posted (sign of psychological safety)
- **Response threads:** People replying to seed posts, not just lurking
- **No fear-mongering yet:** Early adopters should be thoughtful, not catastrophizing

### Wins This Week Look Like
- Members tagged you in threads asking your thoughts
- Someone said "this feels like a real community, not hype"
- New members say "I was nervous about not knowing enough, but everyone's learning"
- One excellent question in #ask-anything gets multiple thoughtful replies

### Potential Issues & Fixes

**Issue:** Crickets. Not many replies to seed posts.
**Fix:** Post twice daily in #first-steps yourself. "This week I'm [small action]." Model participation.

**Issue:** One person posting a lot, others quiet.
**Fix:** Give quiet people a spotlight. Reply specifically to their posts. Tag them: "@[quiet member] you mentioned X—interested to hear more about..."

**Issue:** Someone posts something alarmist or wrong.
**Fix:** Private message first (not public correction). Assume good intent.

## Week 2: Encourage Participation (Sept 22-28)

### Goal
Build momentum. Make it easy for people to contribute. Highlight early contributors. Show that sharing is safe and valued.

### Moderator Actions

**Continue Week 1 Routine**
- Daily welcome for new members
- Daily responses in main channels
- 3x weekly deep engagement
- Weekly synthesis post

**New: Create Recurring Engagement Hooks**
- **Monday:** Post new #first-steps thread with clear prompt
- **Wednesday:** Start a #planning-together discussion: *"What's a decision you're wrestling with? Bring it here—we'll think it through together."*
- **Friday:** Create a discussion thread in #market-discussion: *"What changed this week? Prices? Availability? What did you notice?"*

**New: Highlight & Celebrate**
- Pick 1-2 great contributions per week and celebrate them
- Repost to #wins-and-progress with permission: *"[Name] found this amazing local supplier—here's what they learned..."*
- DM the person: *"Your post in #first-steps was really helpful. People are going to copy your approach. Nice work."*
- This builds confidence and models what contribution looks like

**New: Start a Member Story**
- Find a member who's been active (even quietly)
- DM: *"Your intro really stood out to me. Would you be open to a conversation about your journey? I'd love to share it with the community."*
- This happens by end of week, posts Week 3

### Content Drops

**Monday (Post in #first-steps)**
- Clear prompt: "This week, I'm checking [X]. What's one small thing you're doing?"

**Wednesday (Post in #decision-making)**
- Framework: "How to make a decision when the future is uncertain"
- Example: Real scenario someone brought up
- Keep it practical

**Friday (Post in #resources)**
- Interesting article on psychology, supply chains, or resilience
- Your take: *"Why this matters to how we think about planning"*

**Tuesday/Thursday (Optional)**
- Respond to and repost best #ask-anything questions
- Transform them into bigger conversations

### Metrics to Watch
- **Member posts in core channels:** You want to see 3-5 original posts per member per week (not just replies)
- **Member-to-member conversation:** Posts not tagged @you, just people talking to each other
- **Question depth:** Questions get more specific and thoughtful
- **Cross-channel references:** Someone references a discussion from another channel

### Wins This Week Look Like
- A member brings a real scenario to #planning-together and gets 3+ thoughtful replies
- Someone says "I tried what [other member] suggested and it worked"
- A thread in #market-discussion has 5+ participants sharing local observations
- You can see personality and expertise starting to emerge in who's active

### Potential Issues & Fixes

**Issue:** Members still mostly talking to moderators, not each other.
**Fix:** When someone posts, reply with: *"[Name] what do you think about what [other member] said?"* Force cross-connection.

**Issue:** Same 3-4 people talking, others silent.
**Fix:** Tag quiet people directly: "@[quiet member] you mentioned [thing] in your intro. Thoughts on [thread]?"

**Issue:** Discussions getting too long or branching out of focus.
**Fix:** Start a new thread: "Great question, but this is different from the original discussion. Let's take this to a new thread."

## Week 3: Deepen Connections (Sept 29-Oct 5)

### Goal
Build relationships between members. Create sub-communities. Establish clear community identity. Prove that SAL leads to real connections.

### Moderator Actions

**Continue Weeks 1-2 Routine**
- Daily welcome
- Daily engagement
- Weekly highlights and celebration
- Recurring hooks (Mon/Wed/Fri)

**New: Facilitate Connections**
- Look for common interests and introduce them: *"[Name A] and [Name B] are both interested in local sourcing. You two should connect."*
- Create a #biohoods regional group if multiple people mention the same area: *"Anyone in the Northeast? Want to start a regional thread?"*
- DM people who might naturally connect: *"I think you two would have a good conversation about..."*

**New: Share Leadership**
- Find 1-2 community members who are naturally helpful
- Ask them to co-facilitate a channel: *"You've been so thoughtful in #planning-together. Would you be interested in helping moderate that channel? I'll make you a co-mod."*
- This distributes work and makes people feel invested

**New: Deep Member Spotlight**
- Interview 1-2 members this week (done by Friday)
- DM: *"I'd love to interview you for a #member-stories feature. 15 minutes?"*
- Questions: How'd you find SAL? What are you working on? What's been most helpful? Where are you from?
- Post the feature Friday or Monday with their consent

### Content Drops

**Monday (New: Local Solutions Thread)**
- Start a thread in #local-solutions: *"What resources have you found where you live? Bulk suppliers, co-ops, farmer's markets, community gardens?"*
- Invite replies organized by region

**Wednesday (New: Psychology Deep Dive)**
- Post a slightly deeper piece in #research-library
- Topic: "Behavioral economics" or "How to reduce decision fatigue" or "Building group resilience"
- 3-4 paragraphs, but really good

**Friday (New: Week in Review)**
- Post in #market-discussion: *"Here's what I noticed this week in supply trends. What did you all see?"*
- Synthesis of what community members shared

**Ongoing**
- Keep resource links going (2x per week)
- Keep first-steps framework going (1x per week)

### Metrics to Watch
- **Member-to-member DMs:** You should start hearing people are connecting outside Discord
- **Sub-community formation:** Regional groups, interest groups forming organically
- **Emergent experts:** Clear people who know supply chains, psychology, local sourcing, etc.
- **Turnover check:** Are people from Week 1 still active? (Should be ~70%+)

### Wins This Week Look Like
- A member says "I connected with someone from my area and we're planning to compare suppliers"
- A thread in #planning-together has real problem-solving, not just questions
- Member spotlight gets 10-15 replies (people care about each other)
- Someone says in #introductions "A member here helped me understand..."
- Regional/interest subgroups are discussing in #biohoods

### Potential Issues & Fixes

**Issue:** Some members falling off radar.
**Fix:** DM them (not public). *"Haven't seen you in discussions lately. Everything okay? I'd love to hear what you're working on."*

**Issue:** Cliques forming (old members exclusive to new).
**Fix:** Actively introduce new to established members. Don't let groups ossify.

**Issue:** One type of discussion dominating (all pricing, all psychology).
**Fix:** Intentionally prompt in underutilized channels. Bring variety.

## Week 4: Celebrate & Build Momentum (Oct 6-12)

### Goal
Celebrate early wins. Establish community identity and norms. Show that SAL leads to action and connection. Build self-sustaining culture (less moderator input needed).

### Moderator Actions

**Continue Weeks 1-3 Routine**
- Daily welcome and engagement
- 3x weekly highlights
- Recurring engagement hooks
- Member spotlights

**New: Month-1 Celebration Post**
- Create a synthesis post in #announcements or #wins-and-progress
- Format: *"Here's what SAL community built in 30 days..."*
- Include:
  - Number of members
  - Top themes discussed
  - Member spotlights (3-5 members by name)
  - Wins shared (people who took action)
  - Emerging expertise (who knows what?)
  - Regional connections forming
- Make it feel real and specific

**New: Survey (Optional)**
- Post a quick Google form: "30-day feedback: What's working? What could we improve?"
- Share results next week
- Shows you're listening and iterating

**New: Identify Future Leaders**
- Look at who's been most thoughtful, helpful, present
- DM 2-3 people: *"You've been such a thoughtful presence in SAL. Would you be interested in being a moderator? I'd like to grow the moderation team."*
- Have a conversation about values/expectations
- Formalize role by end of week

**New: Build on Emerging Themes**
- Notice what people care about most (pricing? psychology? local sourcing?)
- Create a focused series: *"Let's do a deep dive on commodity pricing. Here's how to read USDA data..."*
- Lean into what the community wants

### Content Drops

**Monday (New: Member Showcase)**
- Post a member spotlight
- Format: Name, what they're working on, what they've learned, where they're from
- Keep it 2-3 paragraphs, authentic

**Wednesday (Framework Post)**
- Synthesize what the community has learned
- Topic: "Four things we've learned in 30 days about supply resilience"
- Make it collaborative (reference member discussions)

**Friday (New: Week & Month Wrap-Up)**
- Bigger post in #market-discussion
- Summary of supply trends across the month
- Connections to what community members shared
- Looking ahead: what to watch next month

### Metrics to Watch
- **Community size:** Hopefully 50-100+ members by day 30
- **Participation rate:** 40%+ of members have posted at least once
- **Repeat participation:** 70%+ of Week 1 members still active
- **Member-driven discussions:** Most active threads started by members, not moderators
- **Emergent subgroups:** Regional, interest-based clusters forming naturally

### Wins This Week Look Like
- A member says "This community has changed how I think about this"
- Two members are meeting up in person to compare suppliers
- A thread is 20+ messages with 8+ different people (self-sustaining)
- Someone invites a friend: "You need to join this Discord"
- New members say "People here actually care"
- Member spotlight gets 20+ replies
- You see less need to prompt—conversation happens naturally

### Potential Issues & Fixes

**Issue:** Community feels flat or repetitive.
**Fix:** Introduce a new discussion format or topic. Shake things up intentionally.

**Issue:** Engagement plateauing.
**Fix:** Go back to Week 2 tactics. Daily welcoming, personal recognition, highlighting members.

**Issue:** A few people dominating conversations.
**Fix:** Intentionally create spaces for different voices (DM quiet people: "@you seem thoughtful, thoughts on [topic]?")

## Ongoing Operations (After Day 30)

### Rhythm That Works

**Daily (10 min)**
- Welcome new members
- Answer questions in #ask-anything
- Respond to any major discussions

**3x Per Week (20 min)**
- Deep engagement: substantial replies, highlighting, connecting people
- Celebration: highlight 1-2 contributions

**Weekly (30 min)**
- Recurring hooks: first-steps, planning-together, market-discussion threads
- Resource post (1-2 links)
- Week synthesis/summary

**Monthly (1 hour)**
- Community review: membership, engagement, themes, health
- Create bigger content piece or member spotlight
- Feedback loop: iterate based on what's working

### Content Calendar (After Day 30)

**Monday**
- #first-steps: "What are you doing this week?"

**Tuesday**
- #resources: One curated link

**Wednesday**
- #decision-making: Framework or psychology piece

**Thursday**
- #planning-together: "What decision are you wrestling with?"

**Friday**
- #market-discussion: "What changed this week?"
- #supply-chain-alerts: Mid-week or end-of-week update

**As Needed**
- Member spotlights (weekly or bi-weekly)
- Wins celebration (2-3x per week)
- Emerging topics (start new threads as needed)

### Health Indicators (Watch Monthly)

- **Participation rate:** Aim for 40%+ active monthly (posting at least once)
- **Repeat participation:** 60%+ of members from 30 days ago still active
- **Member-driven conversation:** 70%+ of posts are member-initiated
- **Cross-channel traffic:** People referencing conversations from other channels
- **Emergent leadership:** Members helping other members without moderator prompting
- **Growth rate:** 10-20% new members per week (organic, quality)

### When to Scale

Once you have 100+ engaged members, consider:
- Creating sub-servers (regional, topic-focused)
- Recruiting 2-3 more moderators
- Creating a content calendar (external to Discord)
- Hosting live discussions or webinars
- Building a community news/digest

## Key Reminders

✅ **You're building psychological safety.** Every post, every reply, every welcome contributes to this.

✅ **Small actions compound.** One new person introduced to one existing member becomes a relationship.

✅ **Your presence sets the tone.** Model curiosity, vulnerability, evidence-based thinking, and grace.

✅ **Resist the urge to control everything.** Let the community emerge. Your job is to facilitate, not architect.

✅ **Celebrate contribution loudly.** Make people feel seen and valued.

✅ **Remember the mission.** "You don't have to know everything, and you don't have to do this alone." Everything should serve this.

---

**30-day success looks like:** A community where people feel less alone, more capable, and genuinely learning from each other. Where questions are welcome, vulnerability is safe, and small actions feel meaningful. That's your north star.
