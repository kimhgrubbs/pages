## 💡 Key Principles (Reference Often)

**Mission**  
"You don't have to know everything, and you don't have to do this alone."

**Brand Voice**  
Simple. Preparedness. Planning. (Approachable, not distant. Serious without alarmism. Sourced, not personality-driven.)

**Moderation**  
Assume good intent. Extend grace. Correct rarely. Remove only when necessary.

**Culture**  
Facilitate, celebrate, welcome. Don't control or police.

**Success Look Like**  
People feel less alone and more capable. They're learning from each other. They're taking action. They're building community.
