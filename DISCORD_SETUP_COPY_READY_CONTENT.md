```
**Start here:**
```

```
SAL COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

✓ Questions (especially "I don't understand...")
✓ Vulnerability (sharing fears, struggles, uncertainty)
✓ Sourced information (links, data, evidence)
✓ Community wisdom (personal experience, local knowledge)

✗ Fear-mongering or catastrophizing
✗ Personal attacks or shaming
```

## FAQ - INITIAL POSTS (Post in #faq)

### Post 1: Core Questions

```
📌 FAQ — Supplies Are Limited Community

Q: Is this for preppers?
A: No, not in the bunker-building sense. This is for people navigating real economic and supply changes. We focus on collective resilience, not individual survival.

Q: Do I need to know about supply chains?
A: Not at all. That's the whole point. You don't have to know everything—that's why you're here with others.

Q: How is this different from other preparedness communities?
A: We emphasize psychological resilience (not just supplies), collective action (not isolation), and sourced information (not fear). We focus on understanding systems and building community.

Q: Can I ask basic questions?
A: Yes. Especially yes. Basic questions make this a real learning community.

Q: Is this political?
A: We stay focused on supply chains and systems, not politics. We welcome people from all backgrounds navigating the same realities.
```

### Post 2: Practical Questions (Post after initial replies come in)

```
Q: What if I disagree with someone?
A: That's fine. We ask for respectful disagreement with evidence. We're not trying to reach perfect consensus—we're trying to learn together.

Q: How do I get started?
A: 1. Introduce yourself in #introductions
2. Check out #first-steps and #ask-anything
3. Pick a conversation that interests you

Q: What does "you don't have to know everything" really mean?
A: It means expertise isn't required. If you're curious about supply chains, you belong here. The community has collective knowledge. We learn from each other, not just from experts.

Q: What if I'm just starting to think about these issues?
A: Perfect. That's exactly who this community is for. You're not behind.

More questions? Ask in #ask-anything!
```

## BRAND VOICE CHECKLIST

When creating content, verify it has these qualities:

✓ **Simple** — Can a newcomer understand this without expertise?
✓ **Preparedness** — Does this help people make better decisions?
✓ **Planning** — Is this actionable or strategic?
✓ **Approachable** — Does this feel like a friend, not an expert?
✓ **Psychological** — Does this reduce anxiety and build agency?
✓ **Evidence-based** — Are claims sourced? Is alarmism avoided?
✓ **Authorless** — Is the focus on information, not the person sharing it?
