## QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING

### Issue: Not Many Introductions
**Fix:** Post more often in #first-steps yourself. Model what you want to see.

### Issue: Lots of Questions but No Answers
**Fix:** Answer them yourself (first ones especially). Show that questions are valued. People will follow your lead.

### Issue: Someone Posted Something Alarmist or Wrong
**Fix:** Private message them first (don't correct publicly). Assume good intent.

### Issue: Server Feeling Dead/Quiet
**Fix:** Post more frequently. Ask more direct questions. Tag people. Be more visible.

### Issue: One Person Dominating
**Fix:** Give others a spotlight. Tag different people. Start conversations they'll care about.

### Issue: Technical Problem (Channel won't load, permissions broken, etc.)
**Fix:** Post announcement in #announcements with status. Discord sometimes has hiccups—reassure people. Test on your end, explain what you're doing to fix it.

### Issue: Significant Spam or Trolling
**Fix:** Remove the post/member. Private message explaining SAL community values. Use escalation path from Moderation Guidelines.

✅ Assume good intent
✅ Answer questions thoughtfully
✅ Celebrate contribution
✅ Model the values you want to see
✅ Be visible and responsive first week

### Don't
❌ Over-moderate (correction is not your job mostly)
❌ Create an expert/authority vibe
❌ Let spam sit without handling
❌ Ignore signs of fear-mongering (address gently)
❌ Disappear on moderators
❌ Make it feel commercial or corporate
❌ Try to control all conversation
