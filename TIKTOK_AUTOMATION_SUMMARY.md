## CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS (Don't Skip These)

1. **Hook Quality** (First 2 seconds = everything)
   - If people don't watch past 2 sec, post dies
   - Test different hook types weekly
   - Curiosity, problem, surprise, visual - use them all

2. **Consistent Timing**
   - Every Monday/Wednesday/Friday at 5pm ET
   - Algorithm learns your schedule and promotes accordingly
   - Missing a post disrupts the schedule (avoid)

3. **Trending Audio Integration**
   - Check TikTok trending sounds every Monday morning
   - Use audio that's trending (500K+ uses = proven)
   - Don't force audio; let audio shape content

4. **Comment Response**
   - Reply to ALL comments within 24 hours
   - Algorithm boosts engagement of replied-to posts
   - Builds community (people come back for conversation)

5. **Link in Bio Visibility**
   - Text overlay in video: "Link in bio"
   - Bio link constant: SuppliesAreLimited.com/?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=sept_launch
   - Make sure link works (test weekly)

6. **Performance Data Tracking**
   - Log metrics for every post (no exceptions)
   - Weekly analysis identifies what works
   - Adjust strategy based on data, not guesses
