## CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS

1. **Hook Quality** (First 2 seconds)
   - If viewers don't watch past 2 sec, post dies
   - Always start with curiosity, problem, surprise, or visual hook

2. **Consistent Timing**
   - Every Monday/Wednesday/Friday at 5pm ET
   - Algorithm learns schedule and promotes accordingly

3. **Trending Audio Integration**
   - Check TikTok sounds every Monday morning
   - Use audio with 500K+ uses (proven)
   - Adapt content to audio (don't force it)

4. **Comment Response**
   - Reply to ALL comments within 24 hours
   - Algorithm boosts engagement of replied-to posts
   - Builds community loyalty

5. **Link Visibility**
   - Text overlay in video: "Link in bio"
   - Consistent bio link: SuppliesAreLimited.com/?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=sept_launch
   - Test link works weekly

6. **Performance Data**
   - Log metrics for every post (no exceptions)
   - Weekly analysis identifies what works
   - Adjust strategy based on data
