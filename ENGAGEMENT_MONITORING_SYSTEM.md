## PART 1: DAILY ENGAGEMENT LOG (5 minutes/day)

### **Purpose**
Track real-time engagement to identify winning content + respond to audience

### **Daily Workflow** (12pm ET, 3 hours after each post)

#### **Monday @ 12pm ET**

1. **Open Instagram**: @suppliesarelimited
2. **Navigate to Latest Post** (Monday Reel)
3. **Record These Metrics**:
   - Post URL/ID (for tracking)
   - Likes at 3 hours
   - Comment count
   - Comment sentiment (positive/question/negative)
   - Share count (if visible in analytics)
   - Save count (check insights)
4. **Respond to Comments**:
   - Like first 5 comments
   - Reply to 2-3 substantive comments (ask questions, add value)
   - Monitor for spam (delete if necessary)
5. **Log in Spreadsheet**:

```
Date | Post Type | Video Title | Likes (3hr) | Comments | Saves | Shares | Sentiment | Top Comment | Response Status
2026-09-02 | Reel | F5000 Intro | 23 | 4 | 8 | 1 | 100% Positive | "Love this approach to prep!" | Responded ✓
```

#### **Wednesday @ 12pm ET**

Same process for Wednesday carousel post

#### **Friday @ 12pm ET**

Same process for Friday testimonial video

### **Evening Check** (6pm ET daily)

1. Open Instagram
2. Check for new comments on all live posts
3. Respond to any substantive comments
4. Note: Angry/negative comments (rare, but monitor)

### **What to Look For in Comments**

**Gold Comments** (respond + engage):
- "How much does this cost?"
- "Where do you buy this?"
- "Does this work with solar?"
- "Has anyone actually used this?"
- "Best product recommendation I've seen"

**Response Strategy**:
- Product Q: Link to website (use custom link)
- Experience Q: Redirect to testimonials/reviews
- Praise: Thank + ask for follow/share
- Negative: Professional, facts-based response (rarely)

**Spam Comments** (delete):
- Bot comments ("Follow me @randomaccount")
- Emoji-only comments from unrelated accounts
- Obvious self-promotion
- Harassment

## PART 9: CRISIS MANAGEMENT

### **If Engagement Drops Significantly**

**Red Flag**: Engagement rate drops below 2% for 2+ weeks

**Investigation**:
1. Check if Instagram algorithm changed (check announcements)
2. Review posting times (did you change timing?)
3. Review content quality (did your Reels get worse?)
4. Check hashtags (are they still relevant?)
5. Check if your account was shadowbanned (test by searching your hashtags)

**Response Actions**:
- Post more frequently (try 4x/week for 2 weeks)
- Improve Reel hooks (first 2 seconds critical)
- Test new hashtags
- Engage more comments/replies
- Post at different times (try early morning or evening)
- Create more interactive content (ask questions, polls)

### **If Comments Turn Negative**

**Low-Risk**: Product criticism ("This is too expensive")
- Response: "It's premium quality for premium performance. We focus on reliability over price. Here's why [brief explanation]."

**Medium-Risk**: Comparison attacks ("Brand X is better")
- Response: "Different products for different needs. We focus on [unique value]. DMs open if you want to compare specs!"

**High-Risk**: Misinformation ("This doesn't actually work")
- Response: "We'd love to help clarify! Our systems have [X certification/warranty]. DMs open for questions or you can call [number]."

**Critical**: Harassment or dangerous misinformation
- Action: Delete comment + restrict user + monitor for pattern
