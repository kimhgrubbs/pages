## FINAL NOTES

**Realistic expectations**:
- Week 1-2: Low views (building audience)
- Week 3-4: View growth as content compounds
- Week 5-8: Peak engagement as audience solidifies
- By week 8: 5,000-20,000 total views, 300+ signups, 5-10 orders

**Success looks like**:
- Consistent engagement rate 5-10%
- Growing follower count (50+ new followers per week)
- Repeat engagement from same people
- Helpful comments/questions (not spam)
- Click-through rate 2-5% from Threads to site

**Red flags**:
- Engagement declining week-over-week (adjust content)
- No email signups (link issue or not compelling enough)
- Spam/low-quality engagement (community may not be right fit)
- High bounce rate from Threads link (landing page issue)

**If underperforming**:
- Review post timing (different time might work better)
- Test different themes (Week 3-4 data tells you what resonates)
- Increase engagement (reply to comments = more visibility)
- Check link functionality (verify URL is working)
- Assess if Threads is right platform for this audience (may need different channel)
