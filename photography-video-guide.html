Photography & Video Direction Guide

Professional visual standards for product photography, lifestyle imagery, and video content. This guide ensures consistent, premium aesthetic across all marketing materials.

Overall Visual Direction

Supplies Are Limited is premium preparation for high-income professionals. Our photography reflects:

  • Confidence & Control: Not emergency or crisis. Professional competence.
  • Sophisticated Aesthetic: Luxury retail level quality. Mid-century modern influences.
  • Research-Backed Credibility: Show products in real-world settings, not studio isolation.
  • Aspirational Living: Modern homes, contemporary professionals, elevated lifestyle.
  • Attention to Detail: Lighting, composition, styling all refined and deliberate.

Pecron F5000 Photography Direction

Power Systems

Product Photography

Background: Clean white sweep or minimal neutral gray (no clutter).

Lighting: Bright, directional key light. Fill light at 45-degree angle to minimize harsh shadows. Emphasize industrial design and metallic finishes.

Angle: 3/4 front view showing control panel and form factor. Show orange accent details.

Focus: Sharp, detailed product shots. Display LED indicators, ports, build quality.

Lifestyle Photography

Settings: Premium home environments—modern living rooms, tech-forward offices, home media centers, home office setups.

Context: Show Pecron powering realistic scenarios: charging laptop, running security system, emergency lighting setup (but not panicked scenes).

People: Confident professionals, calm demeanor. They're in control, not worried.

Styling: Contemporary interiors. Warm, professional tones.

Color Grading
Warm Orange Tones

Color Temperature: 3500K–4500K (warm, professional lighting)
Saturation: Enhance oranges naturally, not artificial
Contrast: Crisp, high contrast emphasizing detail
Highlights: Retain detail (avoid blown-out whites)
Shadows: Maintain readability and product detail

✓ Do This

  • Show product in real scenarios (office desk, home setup)
  • Emphasize industrial design and premium build quality
  • Use warm lighting creating professional atmosphere
  • Show LED indicators and control panels in detail
  • Include lifestyle shots of people confidently using product
  • Show battery capacity indicators and charging ports

✗ Don't Do This

  • Never show panicked or emergency scenarios
  • Avoid dark, dramatic lighting (too alarmist)
  • Don't overexpose orange (keep it sophisticated)
  • Never show product in chaos or disaster setting
  • Avoid overly bright or artificial lighting
  • Don't hide technical details—they prove quality

Big Berkey Photography Direction

Water Purity Systems

Product Photography

Background: Clean white or very light backgrounds. Emphasis on clarity and purity.

Lighting: Bright, even lighting. Use backlighting to emphasize water clarity and glass construction. Show water droplets and filtration element detail.

Water Hero: Show water in the system. Emphasis on transparency and clarity.

Detail Shots: Close-ups of black filters, stainless steel construction, spigot mechanism.

Lifestyle Photography

Settings: Modern kitchens, dining areas, wellness spaces, yoga studios, contemporary home offices.

Context: Show family members hydrating, cooking with filtered water, wellness routines, children drinking from Berkey.

People: Healthy, satisfied, confident. Family moments emphasizing wellness.

Styling: Contemporary kitchens with natural light. Clean, minimal aesthetic.

Color Grading
Cool, Clean Tones

Color Temperature: 5000K–5500K (daylight, clinical clarity)
Saturation: Enhance teals naturally, emphasize clarity
Brightness: Bright, airy mood. High key lighting.
Contrast: Crisp emphasizing transparency
Teal Accents: Subtle teal color grading enhancement

✓ Do This

  • Emphasize water clarity and purity (clear water is hero)
  • Show water droplets and filtration detail
  • Use bright, natural daylight
  • Include family wellness moments
  • Show the glass and stainless steel construction
  • Emphasize health and hydration benefits through context

✗ Don't Do This

  • Avoid dark or murky water imagery
  • Don't show contaminated water scenarios
  • Avoid overly clinical/hospital-like aesthetic
  • Never show product in cluttered kitchen
  • Don't use cool, clinical color grading (keep it warm enough)
  • Avoid sad or worried expressions (always positive)

LETPOT Photography Direction

Indoor Gardening Systems

Product Photography

Background: White or minimal backgrounds. Emphasis on lush green growth.

Lighting: Bright, natural-looking light showing LED grow lights in operation. Emphasize vibrant green plants and healthy growth.

Growth Hero: Show thriving herbs, salad greens, or vegetables. Lush, abundant plant life is the focus.

Detail Shots: Close-ups of LED lights, water level, seedlings sprouting, detailed leaf texture.

Lifestyle Photography

Settings: Modern kitchens, apartments, office spaces, dining areas, kitchen counters.

Context: Show fresh herbs being used in cooking, salad being harvested, fresh produce being prepared. Year-round abundance messaging.

People: Happy, satisfied, proud of their fresh food. Lifestyle-focused, not technical.

Styling: Contemporary interiors, natural light, food preparation moments.

Color Grading
Warm, Organic Tones

Color Temperature: 3500K–4500K (warm, golden hour-like)
Saturation: Enhance greens and coral accents naturally
Mood: Sunlit, vital, organic
Green Enhancement: Slightly boost green channel without unnatural look
Warmth: Slight warm cast emphasizing life and abundance

✓ Do This

  • Show lush, healthy plant growth (hero imagery)
  • Emphasize vibrant greens and fresh food abundance
  • Show LED lights in operation with healthy plants
  • Include food preparation and harvest moments
  • Show year-round growing (no seasonal limitation)
  • Emphasize self-sufficiency and control over food supply

✗ Don't Do This

  • Avoid wilted, diseased, or struggling plants
  • Don't show empty systems or failed crops
  • Avoid overly clinical grow-tent aesthetic
  • Never show product in messy or cluttered space
  • Don't oversaturate green to unnatural levels
  • Avoid grim or desperate expressions (always positive)

Video Production Guidelines

Cinematography Style

Camera Work

Movement: Smooth, deliberate camera movements. Slow push-ins, subtle pans. No shaky handheld.

Framing: Rule of thirds. Asymmetrical composition. Generous headroom and clear sightlines.

Shots: Mix of wide establishing, medium product shots, close-up detail work.

Focus: Sharp, professional focus pulling. Shallow depth of field for product focus (20-30mm equivalent).

Lighting

Style: Three-point professional lighting with natural-looking results.

Key Light: Directional, creating dimension. 45-degree angle.

Fill Light: Subtle fill to maintain shadow detail.

Backlight: Creates separation from background, adds dimension.

Result: Professional, premium feel. Natural-looking, not overly lit.

Color Grading

LUT: Develop consistent color lookup table for all videos.

Consistency: Match photography color grade for visual unity.

Look: Slightly warm, slightly saturated. Luxury brand aesthetic.

Highlights/Shadows: Crushed blacks, retained highlights. Professional cinema grade.

Editing & Pacing

Cuts: Clean, purposeful cuts on action or natural beats.

Transitions: Minimal. Mostly cuts. Dissolves used sparingly.

Pacing: 2–3 second product shots. Longer on lifestyle/narrative.

Duration: 15–30 second format (optimized for social/ads).

Text Overlay Standards

Font: Playfair Display (headlines), Inter (supporting text)
Size: Large enough to read on mobile (16px+ minimum)
Positioning: Lower third or asymmetrical placement
Animation: Subtle fade-ins. No flashy text effects.
Duration: 3–5 seconds on screen for readability
Color: Brand orange (#FF6B35), white, or charcoal. Always maintain legibility.

Audio Recommendations

Sound Design

Premium, subtle sound design. No generic stock audio. Focus on:

  • Natural product sounds (water pouring, buttons clicking, LED hum)
  • Clean, minimal ambient sound
  • Professional voiceover (warm, authoritative tone)

Music

Subtle, non-distracting background music:

  • Upbeat but sophisticated (not trendy)
  • Instrumental preferred
  • Premium production (not obviously stock)
  • Matches pacing of footage

Production Checklist

Before Shooting

  • Scout locations and finalize backgrounds (clean, uncluttered, appropriate for brand)
  • Test lighting setup and color temperature
  • Prepare styling and props (use premium, contemporary styling)
  • Create shot list organized by product
  • Confirm all camera/audio/lighting equipment is functional
  • Test color balance and white balance settings
  • Have backup power and memory cards

During Shooting

  • Shoot in RAW format (for maximum color grading flexibility)
  • Maintain consistent lighting throughout shoot
  • Capture multiple angles and variations
  • Get both wide and close-up detail shots
  • Record ambient audio and natural product sounds
  • Capture lifestyle moments with real people
  • Take reference photos for color grading consistency

Post-Production

  • Apply consistent color grade to all footage
  • Remove any identifying brands/logos (except Supplies Are Limited)
  • Optimize for multiple platforms (square, vertical, horizontal ratios)
  • Add captions/subtitles for accessibility
  • Test on mobile devices for readability
  • Compress for web (target 50–100MB max for video files)
  • Export multiple formats (MP4, WebM, GIF variations)

Technical Specifications

Photography

Resolution: Minimum 5000×5000px (300 DPI for print)
Format: RAW (original), JPEG/WebP (export)
File Size: Under 200KB for web
Color Space: sRGB for web, Adobe RGB for print

Video

Resolution: 1080p minimum (1920×1080), 4K preferred
Frame Rate: 24fps (cinema), 30fps (standard)
Codec: H.264 (MP4), VP9 (WebM)
File Size: Target 50–100MB for short-form video

Accessibility Requirement

All videos must include captions or subtitles. Alt text required for all images. Ensure 4.5:1 contrast ratio for any text overlays on video.
