Upcoming event teaser. Date, topic, registration link. Professional imagery.

Team moments, brand values, company story. Humanizing, authentic positioning.

Google Ads (Search & Display)

Google Responsive Search Ads

Element Specification Example
Headline 1 30 characters max. Lead with benefit/product name. "Emergency Power for Professionals"
Headline 2 30 characters max. Support headline 1 or add unique selling point. "Tested 24-Hour Backup Systems"
Headline 3 30 characters max. Call-to-action or urgency (optional). "Save $400 on Complete System"
Description 1 90 characters max. Explain value and differentiation. "Premium preparation for high-income professionals. Research-backed resilience products."
Description 2 90 characters max. Add proof points or limited-time offer. "Join 50,000+ professionals prepared for the unexpected. Free shipping on orders $500+."
Display URL Branded URL. Keeps it simple (domain.com/products) suppliesarelimited.com/pecron

Google Display Ads

Square (300×300 px)
Small square. Use for sidebar placement. Product image + brand color accent + CTA text.
Medium Rectangle (300×250 px)
Most common. Product image + headline + CTA. Sufficient space for details.
Leaderboard (728×90 px)
Horizontal top-of-page banner. Bold headline + logo + CTA. Limited space requires clear hierarchy.
Half Page (300×600 px)
Vertical sidebar banner. Product image + headline + value statement + CTA.
Wide Skyscraper (160×600 px)
Narrow vertical banner. Minimal text. Focus on visuals and logo. Clear CTA.
Mobile Banner (320×50 px)
Mobile top banner. Logo + single word headline + CTA link. Minimal design.

Design Standards for All Display Ads:

  • Brand color (orange) accent bar or button
  • High-quality product image (front-center)
  • Large, readable headline (sans-serif, 18px+)
  • 4.5:1 contrast ratio for text
  • Clear call-to-action button
  • Logo placement in corner (not obstructing product)
  • No more than 5 elements per ad
  • Export as PNG/GIF with appropriate DPI

Google Shopping Ads

Product Image
Minimum 800×800 px (PNG, JPG, or GIF)

Image Requirements: Product takes up at least 75% of image area. Clean white background. High resolution. No watermarks or promotional text.

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Facebook Feed Ads

Image Ad
1080 × 1080 px (1:1 ratio)
Carousel Ad (per card)
1080 × 1080 px or 1200 × 628 px

Copy Strategy: 125–150 characters for headline. 90–125 characters for primary text. Leads with benefit, ends with CTA.

Visual Strategy: Product-focused 60%, lifestyle/benefit 40%. Brand colors prominent. High contrast for mobile readability.

Facebook Video Ads

Aspect Ratio
1:1 (square), 4:5 (vertical), 16:9 (horizontal)

Duration: 15–30 seconds optimal. Thumb-stop first 3 seconds.

Audio: Captions required (sound-off viewing is default). Music/voiceover clear and professional.

Call-to-Action Button: "Learn More", "Shop Now", "Get Started" options at bottom of video.

Email Advertising Specifications

Email Width
600px (mobile-optimized)
Hero Image
600 × 300 px

Template Structure:

  • Preheader text (40–50 chars) - draws recipients in
  • Logo/header (100px height)
  • Hero image or color block (600px width)
  • Main CTA button (prominent, orange #FF6B35)
  • Body copy (16px, left-aligned)
  • Supporting CTA
  • Footer with unsubscribe + company info

Font Guidelines: Use system fonts only (Arial, Helvetica, Georgia). No Google Fonts in email. Fallback fonts critical.
