### Q: What if Threads doesn't publish at the right time?

### Q: How do I know if links are working?

## RISK MITIGATION

### Risk 1: Posts don't publish on time
**Mitigation**: Set phone reminders for 5 minutes before each post. Check Meta Business Suite that morning. Have backup plan to manually publish if needed.

### Risk 2: Low engagement / no comments
**Mitigation**: This is normal Weeks 1-2. By Week 3, audience builds. If still low at Week 4: adjust post timing (try 9am ET instead of 10am), change content angle (more controversy, more personal stories), increase engagement time (reply to every comment immediately).

### Risk 3: Negative comments / trolls
**Mitigation**: Follow THREADS_ENGAGEMENT_PLAYBOOK.md "When Not to Reply" section. Don't engage with trolls. Delete obvious spam. Stay professional with skeptics.

### Risk 4: Links don't work or UTM params are wrong
**Mitigation**: Test all links BEFORE scheduling. Verify UTM parameters: utm_source=threads, utm_medium=social, utm_campaign=sept_launch. Retest every 2 weeks.

### Risk 5: Can't keep up with replies
**Mitigation**: Prioritize high-intent users first (people asking about buying). Use templates from Playbook (saves 50% time). Set 30-minute engagement window, then step away.

## SUPPORT & TROUBLESHOOTING

### Problem: "Can't connect Threads to Meta Business Suite"
**Solution**: 
1. Verify Threads account is verified (not a brand account)
2. Use personal Facebook account tied to Threads
3. Check Instagram business account is properly configured
4. Try using different browser or device
5. Contact Meta support if persists

### Problem: "Post disappeared after scheduling"
**Solution**:
1. Check Content Studio - it's still there, just queued
2. Verify permission level (Publisher role required)
3. Check scheduled time (make sure it's in future)
4. If genuinely missing: reschedule post

### Problem: "Engagement is very low"
**Solution** (Week 1-2 is normal, try at Week 3):
1. Reply to EVERY comment immediately (algorithm boost)
2. Thread replies to your own post (keeps conversation alive)
3. Check posting time (10am ET might not be optimal)
4. Verify post quality (are links working? messaging clear?)
5. Analyze hashtag use (Threads has different algorithm than Instagram)

### Problem: "Links getting marked as spam"
**Solution**:
1. Use shorter links (consider bit.ly if URL is long)
2. Don't link to multiple URLs in same post
3. Verify domain isn't flagged (check SuppliesAreLimited.com reputation)
4. Remove excessive UTM parameters (keep to 3 parameters max)
5. Contact Threads support if falsely flagged

### Problem: "Need to change a post after scheduling"
**Solution**:
1. Go to Meta Business Suite → Content Studio
2. Find scheduled post
3. Click edit
4. Make changes
5. Changes apply to scheduled version
6. No need to reschedule
