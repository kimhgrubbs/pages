SUPPORT & ESCALATION
================================================================================

PROBLEM: Posts not publishing on time
SOLUTION: Check Meta Business Suite, restart app, manually publish as backup
CONTACT: Meta support or scheduling tool support

PROBLEM: Low engagement or no comments
SOLUTION: This is normal weeks 1-2. By week 3, audience builds. Increase reply rate.
CONTACT: Review THREADS_ENGAGEMENT_PLAYBOOK.md for troubleshooting

PROBLEM: Links not working or marked as spam
SOLUTION: Test all links, use bit.ly if URL is long, verify domain reputation
CONTACT: Check link functionality, verify UTM parameters

PROBLEM: Can't keep up with replies
SOLUTION: Prioritize high-intent users, use templates (saves 50% time), set 30-min window
CONTACT: Assign backup engagement person or increase time allocation

ESCALATION: If issues persist beyond troubleshooting steps, contact [insert support contact]
