Supplies Are Limited

Comprehensive brand guidelines for premium resilience products. This system ensures consistency across all customer touchpoints while maintaining the sophisticated, research-backed positioning that defines our brand.

"Don't Panic. Pay Attention. Prepare."

Brand Overview

Supplies Are Limited serves high-income professionals (100K+) in the premium resilience space. We are not survivalism—we are sophisticated preparation. Our positioning balances research-backed credibility with aspirational lifestyle design.

Three Product Tiers:

  • Pecron F5000 (Emergency Power) — Orange & Black aesthetic. Bold, energetic, commanding.
  • Big Berkey (Water Purification) — Teal aesthetic. Calm, reliable, trustworthy.
  • LETPOT (Indoor Garden) — Coral aesthetic. Warm, organic, abundant growth.

Design Principles:

  • Premium without ostentation
  • Mid-century modern influences (clean lines, geometric precision)
  • Generous whitespace emphasizing luxury and clarity
  • Research-backed and credible (not alarmist)
  • Confident and measured tone
  • Never templated or derivative

Color Usage Guidelines

Pecron F5000 (Power & Emergency): Lead with orange (#FF6B35) for CTAs, hero sections, and key calls-to-action. Use charcoal for text and supporting elements. Ratio approximately 20% orange, 30% charcoal, 50% neutrals.

Big Berkey (Water): Lead with teal (#3B8E8E) for CTAs and trust-building elements. Use charcoal for text. Ratio approximately 15% teal, 35% charcoal, 50% neutrals.

LETPOT (Food & Growth): Lead with coral (#E07856) for CTAs and lifestyle imagery accents. Use charcoal for text. Ratio approximately 15% coral, 35% charcoal, 50% neutrals.

Backgrounds: Default to white or warm ivory (#F8F7F5) for primary content. Use light gray (#E8E6E2) for secondary content zones to create visual hierarchy.

Text: Primary text always in charcoal (#1A1A1A) on light backgrounds for accessibility. Secondary text in medium gray (#9A9490). Never use text colors with less than 4.5:1 contrast ratio against backgrounds.

Photography & Imagery Direction

Overall Aesthetic

Photography should support our premium positioning with sophisticated, aspirational imagery. The tone is confident and prepared, never panicked or survivalist.

Pecron F5000 (Power)

Product Photography

Clean, minimalist white backgrounds. Bright, directional lighting highlighting the industrial design. Emphasize the metallic finishes, orange accents, and form factor. Shoot on white sweep or neutral gray backgrounds.

Lifestyle Imagery

Show Pecron in premium home environments: modern living rooms, tech-forward offices, home media centers. Emphasize confidence and control, not emergency. Warm, professional lighting. Never show panic or crisis scenarios.

Color Grading

Warm, golden tones. Enhance oranges naturally. Avoid cool blues. Maintain contrast and crisp detail. Professional color grading (think premium product photography).

Big Berkey (Water)

Product Photography

Clean, bright backgrounds. Water as hero imagery—show water droplets, clarity, purity. Use teal/cool lighting accents. Emphasize the glass and stainless steel construction. Show the filtration element as proof of quality.

Lifestyle Imagery

Berkey in modern kitchens, dining areas, yoga studios, home gyms. Show family members hydrating, cooking with filtered water, wellness routines. Professional, contemporary home settings. Emphasize health and clarity.

Color Grading

Cool, clean tones. Enhance teals and aqua naturally. Bright, airy mood. High contrast emphasizing clarity. Professional color grading with emphasis on light and transparency.

LETPOT (Food & Gardening)

Product Photography

Natural, bright lighting showing lush green plants. White or minimal backgrounds. Emphasize growth and abundance. Show hydroponic detail, LED grow lights, sleek modern design. Fresh, organic feel.

Lifestyle Imagery

LETPOT in modern kitchens, apartments, office spaces. Show fresh herbs and vegetables being used in cooking. Emphasize year-round abundance, self-sufficiency, and sustainable living. Contemporary, upscale aesthetic.

Color Grading

Warm, organic tones. Enhance greens and coral accents. Natural, sunlit mood. Emphasize vitality and growth. Professional color grading with emphasis on life and abundance.

Photography Principles

  • Composition: Rule of thirds. Leading lines. Asymmetrical balance reflecting mid-century modern design.
  • Lighting: Bright, directional natural or professional lighting. Avoid harsh shadows. Use fill light to maintain detail.
  • Focus: Sharp, detailed product/lifestyle shots. Use depth of field selectively to guide attention.
  • Styling: Minimal props. Premium surfaces (marble, wood, linen). Contemporary styling matching brand aesthetic.
  • Diversity: Show real people of varied ages and backgrounds using products. Professional models and real customer testimonials equally valued.
  • Technical: Minimum 2400px width for web use. RAW format for color grading flexibility. Professional retouching maintaining natural appearance.

Voice & Tone Guidelines

Brand Voice

Supplies Are Limited communicates with confidence, sophistication, and measured intelligence. Our voice reflects our positioning: premium preparation for high-income professionals.

Key Characteristics:

  • Credible: Research-backed, specific, transparent. We cite expertise and data.
  • Confident: No apologies or over-explanation. We know our products work.
  • Measured: Not alarmist. We acknowledge reality without panic.
  • Sophisticated: Premium language befitting high-income audience. No condescension.
  • Clear: Active voice, specific language. We name things by what people understand.

Tone Guidelines

Headlines: Direct, benefit-focused, confident. "Power Your Independence" not "You Might Need This Someday."

Body Copy: Informative, specific, honest. Explain *why* products matter, not just *that* they matter.

CTAs: Action-oriented, clear outcomes. "Secure Your System Now" not "Click Here."

Error States: Honest, helpful. Explain what happened and how to fix it. "Password must include numbers and uppercase letters" not "Invalid input."

Social Media: Professional yet approachable. Share expertise and insights. Engage thoughtfully, no marketing-speak.

Language Examples

Say This: "Tested to 24-hour operational capacity in emergency scenarios" | Not This: "Works during disasters"

Say This: "Filters 6,000+ gallons throughout its lifespan" | Not This: "Super filtration"

Say This: "Grows fresh herbs, leafy greens, and vegetables indoors year-round" | Not This: "Magical indoor farm"

Say This: "Research-backed resilience for professionals" | Not This: "Survival gear for preppers"

Implementation Guide

Web Applications

CSS Framework: Use custom CSS with CSS variables (documented above) or Tailwind with custom config extending brand tokens.

Font Loading: Load Playfair Display and Inter from Google Fonts. Provide fallback stacks for IBM Plex Mono.

Color Implementation: Use CSS variables consistently. Avoid hardcoded hex values in component styles.

Responsive Design: Mobile-first approach. Test at all breakpoints. Maintain accessibility (WCAG AA minimum).

Email Templates

Layout: 600px max-width (mobile-optimized). Generous margins and padding.

Font Stack: Fallback to system fonts (no web fonts in email). Use safe web fonts: Georgia (serif), Arial/Helvetica (sans-serif).

Colors: Inline CSS. Test rendering across email clients.

Images: Optimized for web (compressed, appropriate dimensions). Always include alt text.

Social Media Graphics

Dimensions: Create templates for each platform's native dimensions. Export at 2x scale for retina displays.

Text Hierarchy: Large, readable text. Sufficient color contrast. Legible at thumbnail size.

Branding: Include logo, brand colors, consistent treatment across all graphics.

Print Materials

Resolution: Minimum 300 DPI for final print files.

Color Specifications: CMYK for offset printing. Pantone conversions for premium printing.

Fonts: Use provided Google Fonts or embed as system fonts in PDFs.

Accessibility Requirements

  • Color contrast minimum 4.5:1 for text (WCAG AA)
  • Focus states visible for keyboard navigation
  • Alt text on all images (descriptive, concise)
  • Semantic HTML structure
  • Form labels properly associated with inputs
  • Avoid motion and flashing that triggers vestibular issues
  • Sufficient line height and letter spacing for readability

Performance Requirements

  • Page load time under 3 seconds (3G connection)
  • Optimized images (WebP with JPEG fallback)
  • Minimal JavaScript (no unnecessary libraries)
  • CSS-only animations where possible
  • Lazy loading for images below the fold
