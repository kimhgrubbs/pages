## COMMON ISSUES & SOLUTIONS

### **Issue**: Post doesn't publish at scheduled time
**Solution**: Restart app, verify permissions, check Meta Business Suite backend

### **Issue**: Links getting marked as spam/suspicious
**Solution**: Use bit.ly short links instead of long URLs with UTM params

### **Issue**: Low engagement on particular post
**Solution**: Pin high-performing post to profile, share in reply to related comments

### **Issue**: Can't keep up with replies
**Solution**: Use reply templates, set 30-minute engagement window, plan ahead for viral posts

### **Issue**: Threads algorithm not distributing posts broadly
**Solution**: Post at optimal times (Thursday posts tend to outperform), use threading/continuation, ask direct questions
