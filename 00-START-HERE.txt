BRAND ESSENCE (Remember This):
================================================================================

COLORS:
  • Primary Orange: #FF6B35 (Pecron power focus)
  • Accent Teal: #3B8E8E (Berkey water)
  • Accent Coral: #E07856 (LETPOT growth)
  • Text: Charcoal #1A1A1A + Grays for hierarchy
  • Backgrounds: Warm Ivory #F8F7F5 (luxury, not cold white)

FONTS:
  • Headlines: Playfair Display (elegant serif, mid-century)
  • Body: Inter (clean, modern, readable)
  • Data: IBM Plex Mono (timestamps, technical)

VOICE:
  • Research-backed, confident, measured
  • Premium positioning (high-income professionals 100K+)
  • Tagline: "Don't Panic. Pay Attention. Prepare."
  • NOT survivalism—sophisticated preparation

LAYOUT:
  • Generous whitespace (luxury feel)
  • 8px grid foundation
  • Asymmetrical, left-aligned
  • 64px section spacing
